Privacy

RuleCheck collects nothing.

What the app does

The entire rules database ships inside the app. Searching, reading, and browsing all happen on your device. The app makes no network requests, so there is nothing for us to collect even if we wanted to.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, or email address.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No record of what you search for.

This website

rulecheck.app is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub records standard server request logs as part of serving the site; see the GitHub privacy statement for what that involves.
