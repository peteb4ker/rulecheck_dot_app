§ 5.6.1 · Special Conditions

Asleep

An Active card that is Asleep is turned sideways. It cannot attack or retreat.

§ 5.6 · Special Conditions

Special Conditions overview

The Special Conditions are Asleep, Confused, Paralyzed, Burned, and Poisoned.

§ 7.3 · Between Turns

Between-turns order

Resolve Poisoned, then Burned, then check Asleep and Paralyzed.
